The Dangers of Fake News

Erosion of Trust

Fake news undermines trust in media, government, and even science, making it harder for citizens to make informed decisions.

Polarization

It often amplifies existing biases, deepening social and political divides and fueling conflict.

Public Health Risks

Misinformation about health and medicine can have life-threatening consequences, as seen during global health crises.
